Pourya Aliasghari - Home Page

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mechanical & Industrial Engineering at the University of Toronto (UofT). I am conducting my postdoctoral research in the Autonomous Systems and Biomechatronics Laboratory (ASBLab) with Dr. Goldie Nejat.

My research interests lie at the intersection of human-centred robotics, artificial intelligence, and human–robot interaction. I study robot learning and adaptation from a human-centred perspective, with the goal of developing multifunctional and adaptive robotic systems that can effectively support diverse users in everyday physical tasks, by observing people and/or interacting with them socially.

I received my PhD degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering (2025) and my MASc degree in Systems Design Engineering (2021) from the University of Waterloo under the supervision of Dr. Kerstin Dautenhahn, Dr. Chrystopher L. Nehaniv, and Dr. Moojan Ghafurian. My graduate research focused on natural and interactive skill transfer from humans to robots, and the social learning of robots. I previously earned a BSc degree in Mechanical Engineering (2019) from Sharif University of Technology, where I first developed an interest in robotics by contributing to building a social robot (called RASA) for children with special needs.

Other than research, I enjoy road and gravel cycling. Check out my cycling routes around Waterloo, ON, and Markham, ON.
