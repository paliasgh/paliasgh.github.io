I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the [University of Toronto's Acceleration Consortium Lab for AI and automation](https://acceleration.utoronto.ca/sdls/sdl0-ai-and-automation). I work in a highly interdisciplinary team with [Dr. Kourosh Darvish](https://acceleration.utoronto.ca/people/kourosh-darvish-f4d7e), [Dr. Timothy Welsh](https://kpe.utoronto.ca/faculty/welsh-tim), and [Dr. Ariel Chan](https://chem-eng.utoronto.ca/faculty-staff/faculty-members/ariel-chan/) on studying user behaviour when teleoperating robots and improving the ease of use of robotic teleoperation in self-driving chemistry labs.

My overall research interests lie at the intersection of Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) and Artificial Intelligence (AI). I study robot learning and adaptation from a human-centred perspective, to make robots accessible to diverse non-expert users for their everyday tasks, by enhancing personalization, transparency and explainability, and ease of use of robots.

I have completed a postdoctoral fellowship in the [Department of Mechanical & Industrial Engineering](https://www.mie.utoronto.ca/) at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/), where I conducted research on task disambiguation and verification of service robots at the [Autonomous Systems and Biomechatronics Laboratory (ASBLab)](http://asblab.mie.utoronto.ca/) with [Dr. Goldie Nejat](https://www.mie.utoronto.ca/faculty_staff/nejat/).

I received my PhD degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering (2025) and my MASc degree in Systems Design Engineering (2021) from the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca) under the supervision of [Dr. Kerstin Dautenhahn](https://uwaterloo.ca/electrical-computer-engineering/profile/kdautenh), [Dr. Chrystopher L. Nehaniv](https://uwaterloo.ca/systems-design-engineering/profile/cnehaniv), and [Dr. Moojan Ghafurian](https://uwaterloo.ca/systems-design-engineering/profile/mghafuri). My graduate research focused on natural and interactive skill transfer from humans to robots, and the social learning of robots. I previously earned a BSc degree in Mechanical Engineering (2019) from [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.sharif.ir/), where I first developed an interest in robotics by contributing to building a social robot (called [RASA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1FS07W4LdM)) for children with special needs.
